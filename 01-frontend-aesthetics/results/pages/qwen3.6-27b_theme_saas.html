The Method

Three Moves. No Chaos.

A meeting set the old way takes an afternoon. The Huddle way takes a moment.

✳ ✳ ✳

No. I

Name the Huddle

Say what the meeting is for, roughly how long it must run, and who ought to attend. That is the whole of your burden.

No. II

Let It Consult

Huddle pores over every calendar, time zone, and standing commitment — and weighs a hundred possible hours against them.

No. III

One Time, Settled

A single link is sent to all. The slot is on every calendar before the reply can reach the last invitee. Done, and done properly.

Entered in the Ledger

Conveniences of the House

Every feature we publish has stood trial with a thousand teams first.

Agnostic Calendars

Google, Outlook, or iCal — Huddle reads them all as one shared ledger and never lets two entries collide.

Time Zone Diplomacy

Across Kyoto and Kansas, the 9 a.m. finds the golden hour. Fair-ish hours for every seat at the table.

Recurring Orders

Standups, one-on-ones, retros — set a standing order once and Huddle keeps it renewed, adjusted, and honored.

The Polite No

Decline or move a huddle and the fallout is handled for you — apologies drafted, a fresh slot found for the stragglers.

Focus Hours, Enforced

Mark the hours you will not be disturbed. Huddle will not schedule into them, and it will say so to the inquirer.

Meeting Minutes, Summarized

When it concludes, a tidy digest of decisions and next steps is filed to the team — so the calendar is the only record you keep.
