Features

Everything between “hey, we should talk” and “see you then”

One smart layer on top of your existing calendars that works the way humans actually schedule.

Smart slot finding

Huddle scans everyone's Google or Outlook calendar and surfaces only the times when every attendee is genuinely free.

One-click booking

No links, no forms, no “check your inbox.” Pick a slot and it lands on everyone's calendar instantly, agenda included.

Double-book protection

Real-time conflict detection means no more surprise calendar collisions — or the silent guilt of overlapping meetings.

Recurring huddles

Standups, 1:1s, retros — set them once and Huddle keeps them moving smoothly even when schedules change.

Time-zone aware

Distributed team? Huddle finds overlap windows across time zones and suggests the fairest rotation for early birds and night owls.

Meeting analytics

See where your team's hours actually go and cut the meetings that don't need to happen. Your calendar will thank you.
