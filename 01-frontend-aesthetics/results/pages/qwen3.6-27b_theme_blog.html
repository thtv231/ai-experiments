Kashmir, India

The Houseboats Keep Their Own Weather

Above the Dal, the mountains turn silver an hour before the rain arrives, and the houseboat owners know it in their bones before the sky lets on. We were told the storm was three days off; it was, in fact, ninety minutes off, and we learned some things about time in a valley where the almanac is written in cloud.

Shimonoseki, Japan

Crossing the Strait on a Boat Slower Than Its Name

The ferry from Shimonoseki to Nagasaki is a vessel that has opinions about its own schedule. Aboard it I ate onigiri under salt wind, interviewed a sushi chef bound for a Tuesday press conference with the sea, and wrote my jottings by hand because the deck heeled at any angle useful to a laptop.

Beiro, Portugal

A Desert Nobody Advertised

North of the Alentejo, past the last sign that pretends to be European, the landscape simply stops negotiating with you. Empty as a held breath. I drove two hours to find one person, ate dinner with three, and understood fully why so many writers have ended their sentences in this exact place.
